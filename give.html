Practical questions

Before you give.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. The Ephraim Foundation International, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 86-3290387. Donations in the United States are tax-deductible in full or part, and no goods or services are provided in return.

How does the money get there?

Funds are converted to Sri Lankan rupees and sent to the Community Leaders who run each centre, who purchase food and materials locally. Buying locally costs less and keeps the money circulating in the same village.

Can I give to one specific village?

Yes. Note the village when you give and we'll direct it there. If you'd rather we place it where it's most needed that month, leave it unrestricted — that flexibility is genuinely useful to us.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Any time, with no conversation required. We'd rather you start small and stay than commit to more than fits and stop.
