Brand board photography direction: natural golden-hour light, environmental
portraits wide enough to show context, warm true-to-place colour, people
mid-action with dignity and agency. Never posed poverty imagery, never flash-lit.

Save at roughly 1000x1000px for the circular frames. Always write real alt text.
