The root cause

The gap isn't the school. It's everything around it.

Sri Lanka has near-universal literacy and free public education through university. Access to a classroom was never the barrier here.

Two other things are. After the 2022 economic collapse, food prices in rural villages outran what families earn, and children started arriving at school hungry — unable to use the seat they'd been given. At the same time, the exams that decide a child's future are effectively gated behind private tuition classes. Families who can pay, pass. Families who can't, watch their children fall behind a year at a time.

It doesn't take much to break the cycle. It takes someone in the village, week after week, with food and a lesson plan.

The programme

The LEAD centre

LEAD — Learning, Education, Access, Development — is a single room in a single village, open after school.

Children come in and eat. They get help with the work their exams will actually test. They get weighed and checked through our children's clinic, so we know whether a child is genuinely growing and not just showing up.

Some centres run daily. Others run once a week. Each village gets the schedule it can actually sustain — and each one costs $222 a month to operate.

How a centre works

Why this holds

Three reasons it keeps working.

The village runs it, not us.

Every centre is staffed by Community Leaders from that village, on a monthly stipend. It doesn't pause when an American team flies home, because it never depended on one arriving. Your gift also puts a paid job into a rural economy.

It treats both problems in the same room.

A feeding programme leaves a fed child still behind. A tutoring programme leaves a hungry child unable to concentrate. Nutrition, tutoring and health screening happen at one table, with the same children, in the same session.

Small enough to see, simple enough to copy.

This isn't a campus or a five-year plan. It's a room, a leader, food and books — the same package, five times over. You can watch your money arrive somewhere specific, and you can fund the next one outright.
