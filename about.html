Why we do this

A partnership, not a plan.

The Ephraim Foundation International began by funding a medical organisation already working among families displaced by Sri Lanka's civil war in the north-east. We didn't design a programme from an office in the United States and take it overseas. We found local people already doing the work and made it possible for them to keep going.

That's still the model. Every LEAD centre is staffed by Community Leaders from the village it serves, and every decision about what a village needs is made by the people who live there. We work alongside local communities and leaders — never above them.

My daughter has always noticed the smallest details. It reminds me that God attends to the minute things in our lives and in the lives of those we help — and that He lets us be the hands and feet that carry them.

Johanna Moses, PA-C, MPH  ·  Founder & Executive Director

Being straight with you

What we can and can't yet tell you.

We're a small organisation. In 2025 we operated on roughly $17,000, given by thirteen people. That's the honest scale of this.

It means two things. First, an individual gift here genuinely moves something — four monthly donors are the difference between a village centre being open or closed. Second, we're still building the measurement that larger organisations take for granted. We're now tracking enrolment, attendance, sessions held and growth screening at every centre, and we'll publish those numbers as they accumulate rather than describing outcomes we haven't yet measured.

If you want to see the receipts before you give, ask. We'll show you what we have.
