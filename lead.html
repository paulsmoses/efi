Learning · Education · Access · Development

One room. One village. Open all year.

The LEAD centre is the whole programme. Everything else we do exists to keep a child able to walk into one.

What happens inside

Food, homework and a scale.

Children come in after school and eat. They get help with the work their exams will actually test — the same help wealthier families buy privately. And they get weighed and checked through our children's clinic, so we know whether a child is genuinely growing and not just showing up.

Some centres run daily. Others run once a week. Each village gets the schedule its leaders and families can actually sustain, which is why the centres are still open years after they opened rather than months.

A centre costs $222 a month to operate — Rs 68,500, covering food, learning materials and the Community Leader's stipend.

Why this holds

Three reasons it keeps working.

The village runs it, not us.

Every centre is staffed by Community Leaders from that village, on a monthly stipend. It doesn't pause when an American team flies home, because it never depended on one arriving. Your gift also puts a paid job into a rural economy.

It treats both problems in the same room.

A feeding programme leaves a fed child still behind. A tutoring programme leaves a hungry child unable to concentrate. Nutrition, tutoring and health screening happen at one table, with the same children, in the same session.

Small enough to see, simple enough to copy.

This isn't a campus or a five-year plan. It's a room, a leader, food and books — the same package, five times over. You can watch your money arrive somewhere specific, and you can fund the next one outright.

Beyond the centres

When a centre isn't the answer.

Most of what we do is LEAD. Two other things sit alongside it, because a family that loses everything in a week can't wait for a school year.

When disaster hits

Flood and fire relief, Christmas gifts, Children's Day. Fast, practical, unglamorous — through our Helping Hands programme.

When a family needs a start

Seed funding for women's micro-businesses, and housing through The Joy House. Income is what keeps a child in a classroom after we've helped her catch up.

$222 keeps one of these open for a month.
